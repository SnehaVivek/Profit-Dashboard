
<h1 align="center">Profit Analysis and Business Intelligence Dashboard | Power BI</h1>

<p align="center">
  An interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using Power BI and Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional trends, and customer-level metrics.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Project Overview</h2>

<p>
This project focuses on transforming raw sales data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, data transformation, visualization, and interactive dashboard development. Excel datasets were processed using Power Query and used to build a Power BI dashboard that consolidates key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, profitability analysis, and geographical performance into a single reporting interface.
</p>

<h2>Tools and Technologies</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Microsoft Excel:</strong> Source data and dataset organization</li>
  <li><strong>Power Query:</strong> Data cleaning, transformation, column formatting, and data preparation</li>
  <li><strong>Microsoft Power BI Desktop:</strong> Data modeling, visualizations, interactive reporting, and dashboard development</li>
  <li><strong>DAX:</strong> Calculated measures and business metrics, where applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2>Dataset Description</h2>

<p>The project uses four tables prepared from Excel datasets:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Sales:</strong> Transaction-level data containing Order ID, Order Date, Product ID, Customer ID, Region, Quantity, Discount, Unit Cost, Unit Price, Gross Sales, Discount Amount, Net Sales, Total Cost, Profit, and Profit Margin.</li>
  <li><strong>Customers:</strong> Customer details, including Customer ID, First Name, Last Name, Region, City, and State.</li>
  <li><strong>Products:</strong> Product information, including Product ID, Product Name, Category, Subcategory, Unit Cost, and Unit Price.</li>
  <li><strong>Calendar:</strong> Date dimension containing Date, Year, Month Number, Month, Quarter, and Year-Month for time-based analysis.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Data Cleaning and Transformation</h2>

<p>
Data preparation was performed using Power Query in Power BI. The applied transformation steps included:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Importing and navigating the source Excel workbook.</li>
  <li>Promoting the appropriate row to column headers.</li>
  <li>Changing column names and assigning appropriate data types.</li>
  <li>Removing unnecessary columns and refining the dataset structure.</li>
  <li>Standardizing text fields and formatting values for consistency.</li>
  <li>Applying rounding and numeric formatting where required.</li>
  <li>Preparing the cleaned tables for data modeling and visualization.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Dashboard Features and Visualizations</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>KPI Cards:</strong> Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Total Profit, Average Order Value, and Average Selling Price.</li>
  <li><strong>Sales by Category:</strong> Pie chart showing the contribution of Electronics, Furniture, and Accessories to total sales.</li>
  <li><strong>Sales by Subcategory:</strong> Breakdown of sales across Computers, Office, Accessories, and Travel.</li>
  <li><strong>Profit by Category and Subcategory:</strong> Comparative analysis of profitability across product segments.</li>
  <li><strong>Sales and Profit by Quarter:</strong> Comparison of total cost and profit across quarters.</li>
  <li><strong>Sales and Profit by Region:</strong> Regional comparison across East, North, South, and West.</li>
  <li><strong>Cost Analysis by Region:</strong> Comparison of total cost and unit cost across geographical regions.</li>
  <li><strong>Quantity by Region and Month:</strong> Visual analysis of monthly quantity distribution across regions.</li>
  <li><strong>Sales and Profit by Month:</strong> Trend analysis to identify variations in business performance over time.</li>
  <li><strong>Customer and City Tables:</strong> Tabular views of customer-level and city-level sales, cost, and profit metrics.</li>
  <li><strong>Interactive Slicers:</strong> Filters for product category and region to support focused analysis.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Business Insights</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Sales Performance:</strong> The dashboard reports approximately 16 million in total sales across 240 orders.</li>
  <li><strong>Profitability:</strong> Total profit is approximately 3.45 million, providing an overview of overall business performance.</li>
  <li><strong>Product Category Contribution:</strong> Electronics accounts for approximately 83.27% of total sales in the Power BI dashboard.</li>
  <li><strong>Regional Comparison:</strong> The dashboard enables comparison of sales, costs, and profits across the four regions to identify differences in regional performance.</li>
  <li><strong>Subcategory Performance:</strong> Computers contributes the largest share of sales among the displayed subcategories.</li>
  <li><strong>Monthly Trends:</strong> Monthly sales and profit visualizations help identify changes in performance and support further investigation of seasonal patterns.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Project Workflow</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Collected and organized the source data in Excel.</li>
  <li>Cleaned and transformed the datasets using Power Query.</li>
  <li>Prepared the Sales, Customers, Products, and Calendar tables for analysis.</li>
  <li>Developed relevant calculations and visualizations in Power BI.</li>
  <li>Designed an interactive dashboard with KPIs, comparative charts, detailed tables, and slicers.</li>
  <li>Analyzed the results and summarized the key business findings.</li>
</ol>
